This piece pulls apart orchestra compensation — base scale, seniority, and the gaps between ensembles — and tries to make a fairly opaque corner of the arts legible to a general reader.

The visualization work was about comparison: holding many institutions side by side so differences jump out, while keeping enough context that the numbers don't mislead.
