## Overview

As a Frontend Engineer, I worked across the design system, homepage, recipe pages, collections, and the discovery flows that connect them. Here I really learned the importance of UX research — making the experience faster and clearer without losing editorial warmth was the constant goal, and typography, motion, and small interactions all had to add up to a sense of polish.
