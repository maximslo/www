## Market Research

### Readers don't want AI instead of journalism.

They want AI to make it more accessible. Like a museum guide or sommelier.

According to 2026 Reuters' Institute Digital News Report, most people think AI will make the news less transparent, accurate, and trustworthy. 

Yet reader behavior is already moving toward it — trust just hasn't followed. That gap is the design problem I needed to close: making AI feel like a tool that serves the reader's relationship with journalism, not one that undermines it.

## What I learned

### Wear the users shoes.

At first, I tried solving AI news selfishly.

The Washington Post fell behind during the print -> digital shift so for digital -> agentic, executives panicked. Throwing anything at the wall to see if it stuck, teams led to build a chatbot and AI-generated podcast.

AI is not a solution to the suffocation of high-quality journalism done a billionare owner.

But the real juice is in how this is beneficial to actual readers. Wins will come from that.
